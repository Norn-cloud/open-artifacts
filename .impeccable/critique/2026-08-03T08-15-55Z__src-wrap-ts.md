## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored for this product. The three-state agent-presence instrument (amber off-dot / accent Connected pill / busy pulse), the batch workflow (N picks → one generate; staged edits → one Apply), and the split-brain frame constraint baked into the IA are all product-specific. Detector: 26 findings (1 warning side-tab at L287 — markdown prose surface, out of live chrome; 25 advisories) — in-live-chrome advisories are ad-hoc radii/font sizes not on the DESIGN.md ramp (dock 14px, bar 12px, guide 10px, chip-remove 18px glyph, .85rem) plus the amber dot (documented exception) and the [data-oa-editable] contract string. No fixture noise. Browser evidence: all states verified live; edit affordance renders (dashed outlines + focus ring confirmed by pixel analysis); a11y tree healthy; 0 console/toast errors.

## Overall Impression

A quiet, honest instrument with one genuinely excellent idea (presence) and one structural problem: the offline onboarding wall and the missing preconditions (watcher presence, non-empty prompt) around the two consequential actions.

## What's Working

1. The presence instrument — three states on one button, tooltip/aria-label/status row in sync, reduced-motion respected (verified live both transitions).
2. Lossless failure design — stash survives reloads (verified), Apply failure keeps the stash, edit-done reports applied/failed counts.
3. Keyboard discipline end to end — focus rings, focus restore, Escape paths, arrow-key picking.

## Priority Issues

- P1 Empty prompts can be committed — Enter/Add with empty input creates a chip (prompt:"") and enables Submit; an empty instruction ships to the agent. Fix: no-op + toast, disable Add until text.
- P1 Apply isn't gated on watcher presence — with the watcher dead the edit queues and the UI spins 120s. Fix: presence warning in the status row at Apply time + shorter ack timeout, or block.
- P1/P2 Offline guide occludes the pick surface every reopen — 277px wall, focus yanked to Copy button, Escape closes the whole dock instead of the guide. Fix: one-line banner, show once per session, guide Escape precedence.
- P2 No way to cancel an accidental pick — compose bar has no un-pick; only exit is leaving the dock.
- P2 Chip remove buttons are inaccessible by name (× + title only; role=list without listitems).
- P3 Native window.confirm is off-register for Apply/Discard.
- P3 Header CLS (toggle 28→104px) + three offline phrasings; detector advisories on ad-hoc radii/glyph sizes.

## Persona Red Flags

- Alex (power user): no cancel; no global shortcut; arrow-key picking undocumented; 120s stall; no payload preview.
- Sam (a11y): chip remove names itself "times"; guide Escape-trap (one Escape exits session); focus yanked to guide Copy; aria-live may double-announce with toggle aria-label.
- Casey (mobile): guide very tall at phone width; tap-to-pick relies on mousemove hover; no safe-area inset on the dock.

## Minor Observations

Pick control looks interactive but is display-only; busy dot can crowd "Connected" text; guide textarea 128px for 4 lines; refreshStash swallows errors silently.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the guide be shown once per artifact (persisted), with offline opens showing only the one-line status?
2. Should the Apply pill itself wear presence state so the destructive-ish action and its precondition share one surface?
3. Should the batch action move onto the floating bar ("Send all") once items exist, collapsing the two surfaces?
