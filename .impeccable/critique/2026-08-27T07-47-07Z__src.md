## Priority Issues

### [P2] Status-page recovery is guidance-only, no in-product action path
- Why: Owner clicking own stale link gets told to "ask the owner" — they are the owner.
- Fix: Add conditional "Your artifacts" link when account session present.

### [P2] Transient confirm/undo timers are invisible
- Why: Undo toast (5s) and armed discard (3s) have no visible countdown; user loses affordance with no signal.
- Fix: Thin depleting accent bar on undo toast; progress on armed discard button.

### [P2] Confirm popover positioning is fragile
- Why: position:fixed captured at open time; won't follow scroll/resize; can clip off right edge.
- Fix: Clamp left to viewport; reposition on resize.

### [P3] Shortcut sheet has no focus trap
- Why: Tab can leave the sheet behind the modal scrim.
- Fix: Trap Tab/Shift+Tab within sheet; return focus on close.

### [P3] Shortcut sheet promises Esc for drawer/dock but handler coverage is uneven
- Why: Documented shortcut that doesn't work erodes trust.
- Fix: Audit and unify Esc handling or narrow the claim.
